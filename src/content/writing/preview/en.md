title: Preview.
---

I wish winter break would never end.

And yet, at the same time, I want the new semester to hurry up and come.

I spent this winter break at my family home. My breaks have always been a whirlwind of cheer squad training camps, CES, short-term programs, and more, so I cannot remember the last time I simply stayed home, spent plenty of time with family and friends, and took things slowly!

Above all, this break was precious because it let me set milestones for the road ahead.

My rough plan for 2025 looks like this:

- Graduate in five years by adding one more year of undergrad.
- Prepare for graduate school admission in the first half of 2026.
- Take an active part in research to find the field I want to pursue.
- Study entrepreneurship and the startup scene.
- Serve as a manager for the 12th cohort of Tech-Review.
- And whatever else I feel like doing along the way.

Looking around, it seems that friends around my class year are each wrestling with their own questions in their own places. Even while busy with their own worries, they listened to mine and never held back their advice, and I want to thank them once again. Sunwoo Choi's advice corner is always open, too, and I will be waiting for the chance to gratefully give back what I have received.

Of course, the plans I have made might change unexpectedly yet again. Even so, I will not easily forget this winter break. I will not forget the nights I spent until dawn searching for what I truly love, looking into this and that, my heart racing with excitement.

Now I am a fifth-year student. (Getting old.) Even so, this year I want to meet many more new people! Anyone whose eyes light up with something they want to talk about is welcome.

Once again, I find myself looking forward to the new semester.
